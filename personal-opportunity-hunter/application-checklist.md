# Application Checklist

## Core document kit (prepare once, reuse everywhere)

- [ ] **Academic CV (PDF, 2 pages)**: education, IEEE publications (full citations + DOIs), projects, OpenWave India, awards.
- [ ] **Industry CV (1 page)** for career fairs and AWS/Citadel-type programmes.
- [ ] **German-style Lebenslauf** (tabular, photo optional) for bonding / connecticum / Werkstudent roles.
- [ ] **Transcripts** (B.Tech, with English translation if needed) + **degree certificate**.
- [ ] **University of Stuttgart admission letter** (shows "incoming MSc" status) and, from April 2027, the **enrolment certificate (Immatrikulationsbescheinigung)**.
- [ ] **Motivation statement master text** (500 words), adaptable per programme: why AI agents/RAG, what you build, what you will give back (OpenWave India).
- [ ] **Financial-need statement** (150-300 words); several grants (KubeCon, PyCon, NeurIPS, EEML, LF) ask for one.
- [ ] **Community-impact evidence**: OpenWave India member counts, events, GitHub repos, talks.
- [ ] **Two recommendation contacts** (faculty advisor + internship manager); ask them early.
- [ ] **Paper PDFs + 1-page extended abstract** of your strongest paper (EEML, HLF, research posters).
- [ ] **Talk abstracts** (RAG in production; building agents; open-source AI communities in India) for CfPs (PyCon DE, EuroPython, FOSDEM devrooms, WeAreDevelopers).
- [ ] **Passport** valid until at least 2030; scans ready.
- [ ] **Visa readiness**: Schengen (if travelling before March 2027), US B1/B2 (book the interview early; long waits in India), Swiss / UK as needed. Residence permit after the move.
- [ ] **Bank/PayPal account able to receive EUR** for reimbursements (PyCon DE, LF, START Hack).
- [ ] **Professional headshot** + 100-word bio.

## Red flags: never do these

- Pay an "application fee", "processing fee" or "registration deposit" for a programme advertised as fully funded.
- Send passport scans to unverified organisers.
- Rely on an aggregator's deadline without checking the organiser's page.
